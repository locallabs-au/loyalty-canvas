Loyalty Studio

A sandbox to try nurture timings before you build them in your ESP.

What is this? Fake customers · real rule logic · rough outcomes

Start here (90 seconds)

Your scamps showed emails in a line (1st purchase → bonus → redeem…). This tool treats that as rules on customer state instead: “if they’ve bought once and it’s been 4 days, send the bonus offer.” You twist the dials, we pretend 800 shoppers go through the program, and you see roughly who buys again / hits tier / redeems — then you lock those timings as the program.

1
Leave the dials Defaults match your scamp (+1 day, +4/+8, +7, +30). Don’t overthink yet.
2
Hit “Run simulation” Watch the scoreboard fill in. Those %s are directional guesses, not truth.
3
Change one dial e.g. move TP2 from 4 → 10 days. Run again. Did 2nd purchase go up or down?
4
Save → try one customer Save timings, then open “Try one customer” and click +1 day to see which email would send.
