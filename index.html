Start here (90 seconds)

Loyalty programs aren’t really a straight line of emails. They’re messages that fire when someone is in a certain situation (bought once, hit a tier, hasn’t redeemed yet, gone quiet…). This tool lets you set those timings, pressure-test them on a modelled audience, and get recommendations aimed at opens or actions — then preview one person day by day.

Channels & engagement

makes the test real

Sends alone aren’t a test — people have to open / view, click / tap, then act (come back or redeem). Add the channels you use, set rates, and map each message to a channel.

Which channel for each message?

How rates work: Open % of sends → Click % of opens → Action % of clicks (return visit or reward use, depending on the message). Fatigue lowers later opens.

What should we optimise for?

strategic

Instead of only asking “send earlier?”, pick the outcome that matters. For QSR that often means getting them back in-store or protecting opens so the next offer still gets seen. We’ll score several program setups and rank them.

How to read this: “Most likely to open” uses your channel open rates (and fatigue). “Most likely to act” uses click → action rates from Channels & engagement.
